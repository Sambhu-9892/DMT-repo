A Philanthropic Bridge for Nepal's Communities

Engage Nepal believes that those who are most disadvantaged in Nepal need not just a champion, but a partner and together we will build a future that our partners in Nepal can face with hope rather than despair. We serve as a philanthropic bridge, connecting the global generosity of donors with the focused, local action of grassroots organizations across Nepal.

We believe that sustainable, community-led impact happens when we empower those who know their communities best. Instead of traditional top-down aid, we identify high-impact local non-profits and provide them with the resources and capacity-building they need to succeed. We ensure that our interventions are holistic and address the most pressing needs of the Nepali people.

Our Story

April 2015: The Mountain Roared

The earth roared, the mountain fell, and the village was gone. Not damaged, not devastated — totally gone. Disappeared, as though it had never been.

Over eight thousand lives lost, tens of thousands injured, and over two million people left homeless. A scene repeated across Nepal.

December 2015: A New Chapter

On December 1, 2015, Ambassador Scott DeLisi, on his first day of retirement from the State Department, took over the reins of Engage Nepal.

We knew it was too much to ask families to worry about preparedness when they were struggling to put a meal on the table, pay school fees, and access life-saving healthcare.

Since Then: Our Mission Continues

Over the past ten years we have worked with our partners in Nepal on close to 100 projects in support of people who needed a helping hand. Our commitment is to the children at risk, the women who have been marginalized, and to communities living on the edge of despair as climate change threatens their way of life.

And when disaster strikes, we are there too. Today, our mission continues. Whether responding in emergencies or helping people find their way in a changing world we do what we have always done. We Engage Nepal.
