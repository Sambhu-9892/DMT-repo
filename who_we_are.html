ABOUT US

Engage Nepal believes that those who are most disadvantaged in need in Nepal need not just a champion, but a partner and together we will build a future that our partners in Nepal can face hope rather than despair. We work with Nepali partners to change lives and save lives. Engage Nepal serves as a philanthropic bridge, connecting the global generosity of donors with the focused, local action of grassroots organizations across Nepal. We believe that sustainable, community-led impact happens when we empower those who know their communities best.

We do not act alone. Instead of traditional top-down aid, we identify high-impact local non-profits and provide them with the resources and capacity-building they need to succeed. By focusing on our Four Pillars - Health, Education, Livelihoods, and the Environment, we ensure that our interventions are holistic and address the most pressing needs of the Nepali people.
