OUR PARTNERS

Engage Nepal works alongside a dedicated network of partners who share its commitment to creating lasting change in Nepal. Through these collaborations, the organization delivers impactful programs in education, healthcare, livelihood development, and disaster response. Currently, we are focusing our efforts to build these organizations into a connected network committed to transparency, accountability, and impact-focused programming. Together, we empower vulnerable communities with the resources, support, and hope needed to build a better future.

Binayatara Foundation

Binayatara Foundation was established in 2018 as a Cancer Care Center focusing the cancer patients of Madhesh and neighboring states of Nepal. It is a not for profit social organization.

Chaine de l'Espoir

Founded in 1994 by professor and cardiac surgeon Alain Deloche, La Chaîne de l’Espoir (France) provides children in countries that don't have technical and human capital to treat them with medical and surgical care.

CIL-Kathmandu

CIL Kathmandu is a non-profit, non-political, self-help organization run by and for persons with disabilities that promotes concept of Independent Living.

Fusion Nepal

Fusion Nepal is a not for profit organization which is registered in Nepal government with the theme; "Build People Up, Help People Grow because People Matter."

Global Peace Foundation

Founded in 2009, Global Peace Foundation is an international non-sectarian, non-partisan, non-profit organization, that promotes an innovative, values-based approach to peace building, guided by the vision of One Family under God.

Visit Website

Gorkha Foundation

Gorkha Foundation is a non-profit, grassroots organization whose purpose is to support initiatives that reduce poverty and inequality by making sustainable improvements to the living conditions of the poor and marginalized in the Gorkha district of Nepal.

Home Enterprise Forum Nepal

HNN is a non-profit social organization. It was formed by the senior artisans of various skills and arts throughout the country. It's a part of HomeNet Nepal which has been active for last 27 years towards promoting social protection of people live in marginalized communities in Nepal.

Nepal Ambulance Service

Nepal Ambulance Service is a not for profit social organization dedicated to improving the pre-hospital response in Nepal through the provision of emergency medical service in collaboration with various organizations which are crucial component of any emergency or disaster response.

Visit Website

Nepal Center for Philanthropy Development

NCPD is a group of highly driven social sector professionals from across the world who are tied by one common thread: our love for the beautiful country Nepal-home to the juggernaut Mount Everest, the warmest of communities, rich cultural heritage and diversity.

Nepal Youth Foundation Nepal

Since their founding in 1990, NYF has supported more than 110,000 children and youth in Nepal. NYF's primary focus is increasing access to high-quality Education, Shelter, and Freedom. We also provide Emergency Relief when needed, responding quickly and effectively.

Nepal Korea Friendship Municipality Hospital

Nepal Korea Friendship Municipality Hospital was established in 1999, in the name of Kyung Hee Nepal Health Center with the purpose of providing various health services especially to the people of Bhaktapur and adjoining districts.

Rotary Club New Road City

Rotary Club New Road City was established in May 2004 under the umbrella of Rotary Club District 325 in Nepal. It is one of the few most reputed organizations for humanitarian and philanthropic services for needy people. It comprises of young and enthusiastic members.

Sagarmatha Children Home

Sagarmatha Children Home is a non-profit social organization established in the year 2003 which provides home for the abandoned homeless and orphan children of Nepal. It was established with the initiation of a group of people who strongly believe their obligation towards society.

Trans Himalayan Environment Livelihood Program

Trans Himalayan Environment Livelihood Program aims to extend its programs beyond and across the Northern belt that encompasses Sikkim, Bhutan, Ladhaikh, and Tibet, which share similar environment and culture. T-Help Nepal is a non-political, non-profit, and non-governmental organization.

The Partners Nepal

The Partners Nepal (TPN) is a local non-governmental organization (NGO) established in 2012. It is dedicated to serving mountain communities through the conservation of fragile alpine ecosystems, preserving cultural heritage, and running livelihood programs in regions like Solukhumbu and Langtang.
