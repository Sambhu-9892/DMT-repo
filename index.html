LIVES.

Supported by the compassion of everyday Americans, Engage Nepal bridges global generosity with local
action. Under the leadership of former U.S. Ambassador Scott DeLisi, we proudly partner with grassroots
organizations across Nepal to drive sustainable, community-led impact where it is needed most.

4 PILLARS OF ENGAGE NEPAL

HEALTH

Ensuring vulnerable communities have access to vital care by supporting preventative treatments, medical responses, and community health camps.

EDUCATION

Empowering the next generation by bridging the digital divide, supporting inclusive learning, and ensuring at-risk children have the resources to succeed.

LIVELIHOOD

Creating sustainable economic opportunities & skills training for marginalized women and men, enabling them to support their families.

ENVIRONMENT

Partnering with communities to build resilience against climate change through sustainable green initiatives, eco-friendly practices, and disaster risk reduction.

OUR PARTNERS

At Engage Nepal, we believe that even small organizations can make a massive difference. Over the past decade, we have partnered with dedicated 14 Nepali organizations to build essential infrastructure, empower communities, and drive sustainable solutions for a more resilient future.
