Supported by the compassion of everyday Americans, Engage Nepal bridges global generosity with local action. Under the leadership of former U.S. Ambassador Scott DeLisi, we proudly partner with grassroots organizations across Nepal to drive sustainable, community-led impact where it is needed most.

OUR PARTNERS

At Engage Nepal, we believe that even small organizations can make a massive difference. Over the past decade, we have partnered with 14 dedicated Nepali organizations to build essential infrastructure, empower communities, and drive sustainable solutions for a more resilient future.

Executive Director's Message

We envision a Nepal where lasting change grows from deep community connection — where local leaders guide the work, trusted Nepali nonprofits carry it forward, and global generosity helps transform hope into progress. Our projects have made a meaningful difference across the four core areas that define our mission: health, education, livelihoods, and climate adaptation.
The funds we raise come largely from everyday Americans who give what they can with open hearts and willing spirits. Their compassion humbles me, and I am grateful beyond measure for the kindness that inspires our work.
Scott DeLisi
US Ambassador to Nepal (ret'd)
Executive Director (Engage Nepal)
Virginia, USA
