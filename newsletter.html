Newsletters

At Engage Nepal, we believe that meaningful change is achieved through collaboration. By working alongside a dedicated network of partners, we are able to deliver impactful programs that improve access to education, healthcare, livelihood opportunities, and disaster response across Nepal.

As we continue to grow, we are strengthening this network by fostering collaboration, transparency, accountability, and a shared commitment to creating lasting impact. Together with our partners, we are empowering vulnerable communities with the resources, support, and opportunities needed to build brighter, more resilient futures.
